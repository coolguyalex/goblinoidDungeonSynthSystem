Goblinoid Dungeon Synth System // DATA FILE ARCHITECTURE — HOW A TUNE GETS BUILT STEP 1 — SELECT Mood The only decision a DM makes mid-session. Everything downstream is driven by this single choice. Dungeon Wilderness Combat (routine) Combat (boss) Triumph Town Tavern Camp ▼ STEP 2 — VOCABULARY /moods/.csv Defines the raw musical "DNA" for the chosen mood — what notes are even allowed, and how they behave. interval_weights Probability that each scale degree gets picked. Tonic / 3rd / 5th dominate on strong beats — not a flat random draw. cols: degree, weight, beat_strength leap_rules Whether a large interval jump must resolve stepwise, and in which direction. cols: max_leap, resolve_direction register Allowed pitch range. Narrow + low for claustrophobic dungeon; wide + open for wilderness. cols: low_note, high_note seed_motifs 3–5 note starting phrases the generator transposes and varies, instead of composing from scratch every time. cols: motif_id, notes, rhythm ▼ STEP 3 — FORM /structure/_structure.csv Prevents one motif looping forever. Defines how the piece unfolds over time — sections, which voices are active, and cadence placement. section_sequence e.g. intro(8 bars) → A(8) → A-variation(8) → B(8) → fade. Ordered list of named sections. cols: section, bars, next voice_activity Which of the 4 voices play (or drop out / drone) during each section. cols: section, voice_id, active cadence_map Forces phrase endings toward tonic (resolved) or away from it (unresolved/haunting) at specific structural points. cols: section_end, cadence_type dial_shifts Optional: scripted recycle-rate changes over the piece (e.g. drone voice locks in early). cols: section, voice_id, dial_value ▼ STEP 4 — MEMORY (OPTIONAL / ADVANCED) /bank/__bank.csv User-curated, generated at runtime — not shipped. When a played motif gets flagged/locked, it's written here and becomes more likely to resurface. locked_motifs The actual note/interval sequence of each flagged motif. cols: motif_id, notes, rhythm flag_weight Increments each time a motif is re-flagged. Higher weight = more likely to resurface at high dial settings. cols: motif_id, weight, last_used RUNTIME ASSEMBLY — 4 VOICES VOICE 1 bass / drone VOICE 2 mid / harmony VOICE 3 lead / melody VOICE 4 rhythm / pulse Each voice reads the same mood + structure files but keeps its own recycler dial — fresh generation (0) vs. favoring its personal bank (100). ▼ TUNE A structured, mood-correct, non-looping piece — assembled live from data, not pre-recorded.
